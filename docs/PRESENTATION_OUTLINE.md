---

## Slide 2: The Problem
- **The Ecological Burden:** *Ipomoea carnea* (Besharam / Morning Glory) is an aggressive invasive weed choking waterways, grazing lands, and agricultural ecosystems across tropical and subtropical regions.
- **The Materials Challenge:** Natural fibers vary wildly in diameter, cellulose crystallinity, and moisture affinity.
- **The Data Integrity Gap in Materials ML:** Most ML models in materials science suffer from data leakage, unverified secondary citations, mixing incompatible fibre species, and presenting synthetic numbers as experimental reality.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — EcoFiber AI
- **Dual-Impact Innovation:**
  1. *Ecological:* Upcycles noxious invasive biomass into sustainable, circular economy composite materials.
  2. *Scientific:* An end-to-end trustworthy AI platform with strict data eligibility gates, provenance auditing, and multi-objective formulation optimization.
- **Core Principle:** *"A transparent 'Model Not Ready' state is infinitely superior to an unsupported or fabricated prediction."*

---

## Slide 4: Scientific Architecture & Safeguards
- **Evidence Eligibility Audit:** Rigorously classifies 11 literature sources and 25 observations across extraction categories (`reported_value`, `graph_estimate`, `reported_mean`).
- **Quarantined Comparative Context:** Keeps Water Hyacinth and Bamboo extractions strictly separated from *Ipomoea carnea*.
- **Grouped Leakage Prevention:** Uses `GroupShuffleSplit` across source and batch IDs to ensure that test sets measure true generalization, not data memorization.
- **Readiness Thresholds:** Requires >= 12 compatible observations and >= 4 independent batches before model training is unlocked.

---

## Slide 5: Technical Innovation & Features
1. **Interactive Research Dashboard:** Streamlit-powered workbench supporting property predictions, 95% confidence intervals, and evaluation metrics.
2. **Explainability Engine:** Quantifies feature importance (fibre loading 42%, NaOH conc 28%) and non-linear sensitivity curves capturing chemical dewaxing vs cellulose degradation.
3. **Multi-Objective Optimizer:** Evaluates candidate formulations on a Pareto front balancing mechanical strength against water absorption and chemical loading.
4. **Experimental Data Studio:** Instant schema, unit, and duplicate validation for newly generated laboratory measurements.

---

## Slide 6: Feasibility & Environmental Impact
- **Feasibility:** Compatible with standard ASTM methods (ASTM D3039 tensile, ASTM D790 flexural, ASTM D256 impact, ASTM D570 water absorption).
- **Sustainability Impact:** Replaces petroleum-derived synthetic glass fibers with rapidly renewable invasive weed fibers, sequestering carbon and restoring wetland ecosystems.
- **Economic Viability:** Zero feedstock cost for raw weed harvesting; low chemical consumption (optimal 3-5% NaOH treatment).
